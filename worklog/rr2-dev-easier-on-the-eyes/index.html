Making rr2.dev easier on the eyes

Oct 8, 2026 — Worklog • Home.

The About page on rr2.dev had grown into a wall of bullets. Today was mostly a cosmetic pass over the site.

What changed

  • Sidebar table of contents. The About page and every post now pin a jump list beside the article on wide screens.
  • Breathing room. Thin dividers between sections, roomier lists, and a different bullet for each nesting level.
  • Homepage intro. One thought per line, with the links in bold so the eye catches them.
  • Favicon. The browser tab now shows my profile picture.
  • Links. A “Notes” entry in the menu, and links to other sites open in a new tab.
  • Content. The About page gained career, projects, talks and working style sections.
