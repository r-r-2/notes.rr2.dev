The Reading Room is a 3D room that holds the books I've read. Google Gemini Carbon 4 started the rebuild, adding real materials, shadows and a 3D window. When I hit my Gemini limits, Claude Opus 5.5 finished the job in Claude Code, with Fable 5.1 advising on the bigger calls. The old room still lives at /v1.html.

What changed

  • The window follows your clock: golden hour by day, and rain, a moon and the odd lightning flash at night.
  • Every glow at night now comes from a lamp you can see. The wood is warmer and the posters brighter.
  • Click a book and it glides up to you. Table books finally lie cover-up.
  • You walk in through the door, and the room starts on the fast setting so phones keep up.

A test tool

/verify-room runs 12 checks in headless Chrome and reports only what fails. Its second mode made this video: it films both rooms frame by frame in sync, and ffmpeg stacks them together.

What took a few rounds

Mirrored table covers, a window crowded with trees, and two glitches in the video.

What I learnt

  • Say "talk to me first". When I didn't, Claude once built a bigger feature than I wanted, and we had to undo it.
  • A rewrite quietly breaks things that used to work. A test I can run on demand catches them, and putting old bugs back in proved it works.
  • Switching models mid-project was painless. The repo's notes (AGENTS.md and GOTCHAS.md) carried the context from Gemini to Claude.
  • Less was better. Removing the mist, the dust and most of the trees made the room look more real.
  • Headless tests miss things. The black blocks and the stuck mouse only showed up in my own browser.
