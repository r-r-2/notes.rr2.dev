Before moving out of a short-term rental, I scanned it with my iPhone and rebuilt it in 3D. You can walk through it, open the doors and switch on the lights at rooms.rr2.dev.

Links

  • Site: rooms.rr2.dev
  • Repo: github.com/r-r-2/rooms
  • Music: "Trap Banger" by Alex Morgan, from Pixabay

How it came together

  • I scanned it with Scaniverse, a free app that uses the iPhone's LiDAR sensor.
  • The raw scan was too heavy for a phone browser, so I used it as a reference. Claude wrote a Three.js model from simple boxes, and I checked each room against the scan and sent corrections.
  • Most of the time went into details: sliding doors, the glass partition, floor-length blinds, the balcony grill and a mirrored almirah.

What you can do

  • Look at it from above, take a one-minute walkthrough, or walk around yourself.
  • Doors, lights, curtains, blinds, the fridge and the TV all work.
  • Turn on room sound (fridge hum, AC, traffic), all generated in code.

Stack

  • Three.js with no build step.
  • Hosted on Cloudflare Pages, deployed from GitHub.
  • Cookieless Google Analytics.
  • The launch video is filmed from the site frame by frame and cut to the beat in HyperFrames. It ends on the end card from my design system.

I did most of this from the Claude app on my phone, while travelling and walking around a mall. I used Claude Opus 5.5 for all of it.
