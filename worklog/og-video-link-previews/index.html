This is a mockup of a Messages thread. Scroll the thread to see all the cards. Each card plays the real video from that site.

The resume card uses these tags on resume.rr2.dev:

<meta property="og:image" content="https://resume.rr2.dev/og.png"> <meta property="og:video" content="https://resume.rr2.dev/og-video.mp4"> <meta property="og:video:type" content="video/mp4"> <meta property="og:video:width" content="1280"> <meta property="og:video:height" content="720">

To test a preview, send a URL such as https://rr2.dev/?v=1 in a chat. The ?v=1 part stops the app from using an old preview.

Apps that play the video

  • Play the video: Messages, Discord, Telegram and Slack. Messages starts the video without sound and repeats it.
  • Show only the image: X, LinkedIn, Facebook and WhatsApp.

Each site has an og:video tag for the chat apps. Each site also has an og:image tag for all other apps.

How I made the videos

  • Phones and Reading Room: I recorded the real 3D sites, one frame at a time.
  • Resume and notes: I cut short parts from their launch videos.
  • rr2.dev: I made a new animation of my avatar.

All videos are 1280×720 and have no sound. The sizes are from 132 KB (rr2.dev) to 3.3 MB (Reading Room).
