How I learn new things

  1. Ask AI to teach you

    “Explain it like I'm five.”“Break it down from first principles.”“Rewrite it in Simplified Technical English.”

  2. Follow the experts

    Find the best people in the field you want to learn, and follow their work on X, Substack, blogs or YouTube.

  3. See what's working now

    The last30days skill sums up what people on Reddit, X and the web are saying about any topic this month. github.com/mvanhorn/last30days-skill ↗

  4. Start building

    Don't wait until you've studied everything or feel ready. Build something small and learn as you go.
