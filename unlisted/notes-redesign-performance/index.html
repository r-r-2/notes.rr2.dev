What the notes.rr2.dev redesign costs in speed

The old and new site, tested on four network speeds.

12 Oct 2026 · Back to the redesign post

Short answer: the first visit downloads about 40KB more, and text shows up 0.05–0.3 seconds later. Photos, video and repeat visits barely change. No lightweight mode needed.

Photos and video

  • Photos arrive at the same time as before.
  • The video's poster shows up to 0.75 s later on slow 3G. Playback is unchanged.
  • No layout jumps, and the page never freezes on a phone.

Why no "lite" toggle

  • The cost is on the first visit, before anyone can press a toggle.
  • After that, everything is cached.
  • Bigger wins exist: lazy-load photos, respect data-saver mode, and use lighter analytics. Google Analytics alone adds about 155KB, four times the redesign.

How it was tested

Old and new site served like GitHub Pages, on six kinds of page and four throttled connections in Chrome, from Wi-Fi to slow 3G on a slowed-down phone. Three runs each, medians shown. Analytics was blocked in every run.
